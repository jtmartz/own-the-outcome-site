Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

1. What this covers

This policy explains what information Own the Outcome collects through our website and client portal, how it's used, and how it's protected. It applies to visitors booking a consultation, submitting a request, and clients using the portal.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you interact with the site, we collect:

  • Contact and business details you submit through forms — name, business name, email, phone, website, and a description of your business and what you need.
  • Account information if you become a client — your login email and an encrypted password, managed securely through our authentication provider (we never see or store your password in plain text).
  • Service and request history — consultations booked, service requests, messages, referrals, and notes tied to your account, so we can track and follow up on your work.
  • Payment information — we do not collect or store your card details ourselves. Payments are processed by Stripe, and Stripe's own privacy policy governs how they handle that data.

3. How we use your information

We use the information above to schedule and deliver consultations, scope and build the tools you request, communicate with you about your account and services, and improve how we run the business. We don't sell your information to third parties.

4. Who we share it with

We use a small number of trusted service providers to run this site and business:

  • Supabase — hosts our database and handles account login/authentication.
  • Stripe — processes payments; we don't see or store your full card details.
  • Netlify — hosts the website itself.
  • Resend (or our email provider) — sends account and booking-related emails on our behalf.

These providers only receive what they need to perform their specific function and are not permitted to use your data for their own marketing.
